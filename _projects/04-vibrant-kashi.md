**Code:** [github.com/mishraShilpa-28/Vibrant-Kashi](https://github.com/mishraShilpa-28/Vibrant-Kashi)
· **Tech:** React, Vite, React Router, Node.js, Express, MongoDB, JWT

**The idea:** a visitor to Varanasi should find everything in one place: places to visit and their history,
places to stay and eat, the best-rated spots in each category, and how to get there.

**What I built (solo, 2024):**

- Pages for the ghats, temples, museums, historic places, hotels, restaurants, events and festivals, and adventure activities.
- Embedded Google Maps for each category, so visitors can see where places are and get directions.
- Visitor reviews with star ratings.
- Sign-up and login with an Express and MongoDB backend, passwords hashed with bcrypt and sessions with JWT.

**Next:** a 3D scrolling experience and a live deployment.
