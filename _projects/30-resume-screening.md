## The problem

Recruiters read thousands of resumes by hand. This tool extracts key details and suggests the right job category.

## What it does

- **Resume parsing:** extracts contact details, skills and education with NLP.
- **Categorisation:** a machine-learning model assigns each resume to a job category (Kaggle resume dataset).
- **Job recommendation** and a web app to upload a resume and see the result.

## What I learned

- Turning text into features (TF-IDF) and training a classifier.
- This project connects directly to my recruiting work: I know what screeners look for.
