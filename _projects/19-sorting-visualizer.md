## The problem

Sorting algorithms are easier to understand when you can see every comparison and swap.

## What it does

- **Four algorithms:** bubble, merge, quick and heap sort.
- **Step-by-step animation:** each algorithm records its comparisons and swaps, then replays them through the Redux store.
- **Controls** for array size and speed; deployed on GitHub Pages.

## What I learned

- Why merge and quick sort beat bubble sort on large arrays, seen rather than memorised.
- Separating the algorithm (pure logic) from the animation (UI state in Redux).
