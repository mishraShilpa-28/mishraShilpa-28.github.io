**Code:** [GitHub](https://github.com/mishraShilpa-28/customer-churn-prediction) · **Tech:** Python, pandas, scikit-learn, XGBoost

## The problem

Keeping a customer is cheaper than finding a new one. Predicting who will leave lets a company act first.

## What it does

- **Data:** 7,043 customers of a California telecom company, with demographics, services and tenure.
- **Five models compared:** XGBoost, logistic regression, random forest, decision tree and naive Bayes.
- **Exploratory analysis** with charts of who churns and why.

## What I learned

- Cleaning and encoding real business data.
- Comparing models fairly and explaining results to a non-technical audience.
