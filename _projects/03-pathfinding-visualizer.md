**Code:** [GitHub](https://github.com/mishraShilpa-28/path-finding-visualizer)
· **Tech:** JavaScript, HTML, CSS, Express

An interactive grid where you place a start, a target and walls, then watch pathfinding algorithms
(Dijkstra, A*, breadth-first, depth-first and bidirectional search) explore it. A recursive-division maze
generator builds puzzles to solve.
