## The problem

Restaurants need to manage menus and orders online, and customers want to search, order and pay quickly.

## What it does

- **Accounts with email verification** (6-digit code), password reset and admin or user roles.
- **Restaurant and menu management** with image uploads.
- **Cart, Stripe payments and webhooks** that update order status.
- **Search and filters**, order tracking, loading skeletons and dark mode; typed with TypeScript and validated with Zod.

## What I learned

- TypeScript across a full-stack app, and validating input with Zod schemas.
- Webhooks: letting Stripe tell the server when a payment succeeds.
- Lightweight state management with Zustand.
