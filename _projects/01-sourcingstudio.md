## Why I built it

As a recruiter I searched for jobs for 15-20 clients every day, across healthcare, biotech, supply chain and finance.
Each search meant typing long Boolean strings by hand. One missing quote or bracket broke the search, and LinkedIn
quietly cuts off strings that are too long. Switching from one client's field to another meant starting again.

## What it does

- **Five-step builder:** country and city, job titles and skills, seniority to exclude, salary floor and visa rules,
  each switched on or off with a click.
- **Three outputs from one setup:** a LinkedIn search string with a length meter, a Google X-ray search that reads the
  full text of job posts, and one-click searches on NHS Jobs, Workday, Greenhouse, Lever, Indeed and Reed.
- **Visa and clearance filter:** removes roles that need security clearance or cannot sponsor visas.
- **Saved profiles:** four ready-made domain profiles, plus your own, exported as JSON.
- **Job tracker:** log good jobs and copy them to Google Sheets or download a CSV.
