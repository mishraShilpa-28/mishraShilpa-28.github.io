## The problem

Small instructors need a simple way to sell courses, and students need one place to learn and see their progress.

## What it does

- **Two roles:** instructors create, edit and publish courses and lessons; students browse, enrol and learn.
- **Stripe checkout** to buy a course; free preview lessons for everyone.
- **Progress tracking** per lesson and a "My learning" page.
- **Search, filter and sort** courses; instructor dashboard with revenue; light and dark mode.

## What I learned

- Payment flows: creating a checkout session and confirming the purchase on the server.
- Storing and streaming media through Cloudinary.
- Protecting routes by role and by enrolment.
