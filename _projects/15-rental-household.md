## The problem

Renting furniture and appliances is often handled over phone calls. The site lists items by category and takes payment online.

## What it does

- **Admin dashboard** to create categories and products with photos.
- **User dashboard** with profile and orders.
- **Search, category pages and product details**, cart and **Braintree payments**.
- **JWT login** with protected admin and user routes.

## What I learned

- Designing a REST API for products, categories and orders.
- Uploading files to an Express server and serving them back.
- Integrating a payment gateway's drop-in UI.
