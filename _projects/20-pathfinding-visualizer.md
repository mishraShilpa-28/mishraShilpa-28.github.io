## The problem

Graph search is abstract on paper. On a grid you can watch each algorithm explore.

## What it does

- **Algorithms:** Dijkstra, A*, breadth-first, depth-first and bidirectional search.
- **Draw walls and weights**, move the start and target, and generate recursive-division mazes.
- **Animated search and final path.**

## What I learned

- How weighted and unweighted searches differ, and why A* uses a heuristic.
- Animating many DOM updates smoothly.
