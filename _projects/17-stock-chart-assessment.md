## The problem

The brief: list the companies in a provided CSV file and, when one is clicked, show a chart of its data.

## What it does

- **Loads and parses the CSV** in the browser with PapaParse.
- **Company list** on the left; clicking one draws its chart with Chart.js.
- **Responsive layout**, deployed on GitHub Pages.

## What I learned

- Working to a written brief and a deadline, the way a real assessment works.
- Parsing and grouping raw data before charting it.
